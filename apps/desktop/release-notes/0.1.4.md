# UmaLytics Desktop 0.1.4 beta

- Player profiles are redesigned: a short header, one row of stats, and Umas and Matches on tabs with 8 rows each.
- Badges in player profiles now show their icon next to their name.
- Season / All-time can be switched from any player profile, and your tab stays open when you switch.
- Match history shows the date of each match.
- Private players open on their match history.
- Faster startup: the race and skill catalog loads more quickly.
- Changing a captain or team no longer reloads player stats.

Beta installers are unsigned. Windows SmartScreen may require More info → Run anyway when manually launching an installer. In-app updates are verified with the Tauri updater signing key.
